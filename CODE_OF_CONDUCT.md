# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Oddly is an open-source project and we want it to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive space for everyone who participates.

We are committed to providing an environment where people can contribute, ask questions, share ideas, and collaborate without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate of others.
- Communicate constructively.
- Assume good intentions while remaining open to feedback.
- Focus discussions on ideas, code, documentation, and project goals.
- Give useful and actionable feedback.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Respect different perspectives and experiences.
- Help create an environment where newcomers feel comfortable contributing.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful comments.
- Threats or deliberate intimidation.
- Sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Sustained personal criticism unrelated to the project.
- Deliberate attempts to disrupt project discussions or contributions.
- Any other behavior that would reasonably make the project unsafe or hostile for others.

## Project Discussions

Technical disagreements are normal and welcome.

However, disagreements should remain focused on the problem being discussed rather than becoming personal.

When disagreeing:

- Explain your reasoning.
- Provide evidence or examples when useful.
- Listen to alternative approaches.
- Avoid personal attacks.
- Be willing to revise your position when new information is presented.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior, including:

- Asking someone to stop inappropriate behavior.
- Removing inappropriate comments or contributions.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently restricting participation when necessary.

The response will depend on the nature and severity of the behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainer privately.

When reporting an incident, provide as much relevant information as possible, including:

- What happened.
- When it happened.
- Where it happened.
- Any relevant links or screenshots.
- The GitHub usernames of people involved, if applicable.

Please avoid publicly escalating or reposting the incident while it is being reviewed.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues.
- Pull requests.
- Discussions.
- Project-related communication.
- Other spaces where someone is representing the Oddly project.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is inspired by common open-source community standards and has been adapted for the Oddly project.
